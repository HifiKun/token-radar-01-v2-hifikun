How the model works — read it before you trust a number

implied = TVL × market multiple × growth × maturity

  • Market multiple — the median mcap / TVL of the live-token protocols in the same category, read from this snapshot. A category with fewer than three live comparables takes the median across all of them (0.21). Every multiple is clamped to 0.05–1.00, so one thinly-traded outlier cannot set the price. The table below is the model’s own output, not a hand-picked list.
  • Growth — the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −40%..+60%, weighted 0.50: 1 + growth/100 × 0.50. A week of +4,448% is a data quirk, not a trend, so it is capped.
  • Maturity — 0.80 + min(age, 540)/540 × 0.20, from the day DefiLlama began tracking the protocol; proven for 18 months counts as fully proven. Age unknown counts as the board’s median age.
  • Readiness (0–100) — the same three figures, combined: half TVL scale (log scale, $1M to the board’s largest TVL), three tenths growth, two tenths age. It never moves the rank, which the implied value alone sets.

The board ranks pre-token protocols only. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of a token that already trades are left out — WBTC is Bitcoin on Ethereum, not a protocol still waiting on a launch — and so are versioned pools, exchange products and CEXs. Protocols whose token already trades are shown in their own tab at the market's own value, never mixed into the ranks. “Records no token” is what DefiLlama's record says; a protocol on this board may still have a token its record does not carry. Nothing here predicts whether or when a token is issued.
